# Flood-Prediction - Datasets used

## Rainfall

### IMD Public APIs
District/state rainfall, rainfall forecasts and river-basin quantitative precipitation forecasts (QPF).
Use recent rainfall plus Day-1/Day-2 basin rainfall forecasts as model inputs. IMD documents APIs such as districtrainfall, staterainfall and basinqpf. 

### CWC Flood Forecast portal
Near-real-time river water levels, flood condition, short-range forecasts and station information.
Use CWC stations as labels or validation data. CWC’s network covers hundreds of flood-forecasting stations and reservoirs
